# Studio app (all six steps)

Scope: the whole studio web app (shell, Story, Script, Sketch, References, Scenes, Film, Keys). Visitor mode: Operate. Desktop only, built at 1440.

Audience and job: a non-technical grandchild making one family film over a few evenings. They complained it was too busy and hard to follow. Each screen must say where they are, what is next, what is stale and what it costs.

Constraints: plain words; every action shows "Free" or its price; paid actions confirm. Amharic beside English.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Progress woven like a tibeb border: each approved step adds its band of colour. Refuses the grey Linear admin with a purple accent and dots everywhere.

OWN-WORLD: White cotton ground, ink #1d1d1b, one warm-white rail. Tibeb colours do jobs only: green approved, gold current/ready, red needs you or stale, blue selection and links. Woven geometric strips (CSS diamonds/zigzags), 1px rules, no shadows on cards, square-ish 6px corners.

STORY: The maker sees the film's title, the six steps with the woven band showing how far they are, and on each page one clear next action.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left rail 288px: film title (no label), the six steps as numbered rows with a green/gold/outline number, the vertical tibeb band on the rail's right edge with one segment per step; footer with free limits, a spending bar and Keys. Main: Step n of 6, big page title, one-line lead, primary action top right in ink black; on Scenes a woven 11-segment strip with a legend above the scene grid.

FORM: Tibeb border, ranked 1 of my 7 (impeccable's pick). seed:b34fcb8be4c96315
