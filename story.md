That summer in Grade 9, I Robel was walking to Samuel’s house when I heard footsteps rushing up behind me. Before I could turn around, one of the robbers grabbed me from behind.

I tried to pull away, but another robber stepped in front of me and blocked my path. I was caught between them, scared and unsure what to do as they robbed me.

After they ran off, I got somewhere safe. Samuel had been worried because he hadn’t heard from me in weeks, so I called him using my mom’s phone.

When he heard I was okay, we talked about what had happened. Soon we were laughing together about how my trip to his house had turned into such a wild story.
